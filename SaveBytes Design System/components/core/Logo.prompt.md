The Split byte logo — mark, horizontal or stacked lockup — with the brand's intro, loader and hover motion; use instead of spinners and anywhere the brand mark appears in UI.

```jsx
<Logo lockup="horizontal" size={28} hoverBreathe />
<Logo lockup="horizontal" tone="health" size={28} />
<Logo size={96} animate="intro" />
<Logo size={24} animate="loader" />
```

- Colourways only: brand (blue + navy), navy, white (on navy/blue/photo). Product lockups recolour the right half.
- For static artwork (docs, OG images) prefer the master SVGs in `assets/logos/`.
- Never rotate, skew, outline, shadow, or put in a circle.
